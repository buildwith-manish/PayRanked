Where does $100,000 go the furthest? We ranked all 50 states by purchasing power. Comment your state below!

HOW THIS WAS CALCULATED
Each state's value is $100,000 divided by its Regional Price Parity (RPP) index, divided by 100. An index above 100 means prices there are higher than the US average, so the same salary buys less. Data year: 2024.

Ranks follow BEA's published index, which is given to one decimal. States tied at that precision share a rank (Iowa and Oklahoma at #3, Kansas and Nebraska at #10). Dollar figures use BEA's unrounded index and are rounded to the nearest $100. Arkansas and Mississippi are about $20 apart, which is effectively a tie.

An RPP index is an estimate of price levels, and it measures prices, not what any one household spends.
